[h1]Description, or something like this[/h1]
Put transparency on a cup and it becomes [u][b]the cup[/b][/u], put transparency on a bottle and it becomes [u][b]the bottle[/b][/u], put transparency on a teapot and it becomes [u][b]the teapot[/b][/u]... be transparent, my friend.

[h1]Content[/h1]
[u][b]Let me be clear[/b][/u]
[list]
[*]Adds transparency to the Sports Car (for sale in the Peacekeeper store) and allows to see our character inside, our character smiles when he is inside and by default, he is happy to drive a nice sports car.
[*]Adds transparency to some Mechs models that did not have transparency, and gives a little more transparency to those that already had it.
[*]Adds transparency to some helmets, 30 in total (I think (⌐■_■) ), all other helmets, hats, etc. do [u]not require transparency[/u].
[*]Disables the use of the Mech Pilot helmet inside the Mechs.
[/list]

[h1]Things to know[/h1]
- Some helmets have undergone slight modifications, such as [b]enlarging[/b] their glass screens to show more of the player's face (so we can see their eyes and/or smiles when we use those gestures), the shape of the helmets themselves has also been slightly modified to eliminate that [b]big head[/b] effect that some helmets give.

- Of the racial helmets, only those of the human race have received transparency, or at least only those that can receive it, if they do not have a glass screen, they do not need it... the rest of the races do not need transparency because they already show the player's face.

- Some helmets have decorative glasses, i.e., they are not worn over the eyes, but above the forehead, those have also received transparency, just because.
