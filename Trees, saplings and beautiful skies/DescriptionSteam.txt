[h1]Description, or something like this[/h1]
Trees, trees for everyone, saplings for everyone, and some nice, crazy, exotic skies, and that's it.
(ﾉ◕ヮ◕)ﾉ*:･ﾟ✧

[h1]Content[/h1]
I'm not going to make a list, but I think this should cover all the content:

[list][*][b]Restore unused trees[/b] (yes, another mod that restores things that are in the files but are not used).

[*][b]Added customized versions[/b] (modified from originals) of existing and restored trees.

[*]More variety of trees in all biomes and microbiomes, all following a logical pattern, but indeed there are many more trees everywhere (think jungle, though not as thick).

[*]Trees, their stems and foliage, [b]can spawn a variety of colors[/b], giving more visual variety on each planet you visit.

[*]The same as above, [b]but with the terrain[/b], you can find a typical brown soil, or green, blue, etc., randomly.

[*][b]All trees give saplings[/b], even those that did not.

[*]Icons for saplings ranging from original (already made by CF but as they were not used...), modified (my variants) and fully customized in some cases where the icon was nonexistent (ok, I'm not a spriter, don't expect wonders).

[*][b]Colors of the most varied skies[/b] (some examples of colors can be seen on the screens), some a little crazier, others more exotic, hundreds of varieties (Thanks to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198304370154/myworkshopfiles/] SentientSupper[/url], credits go to him for this part).

[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2422986190] Futara's Dragon Pixel Full Bright Shader[/url] [b][u]support[/u][/b], some trees have details that shine at night, some specific flower, some kind of material coming from the tree, etc, thanks to the support of this mod we can see some beautiful things at night or in darker hours.
[b]NOTE[/b]: [u]It is not necessary to use[/u] it if you do not want to.[/list]

I don't know, maybe I'm missing something, in general terms, that should cover all the content.

[h1]Things to know[/h1]
If you use this mod, you don't need these, they do the same thing, but more:
[list][*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2613254568] Vanilla World Variety[/url] (My inspiration for making this mod)
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2963768209] Restore Brain Trees with Eye[/url]
[*][b]Mods that restore tentacle drops[/b] from trees in that biome (there's no incompatibility anyway, just the part of this mod's code that makes tentacles drop would become redundant, these "trees" also drop saplings).

[*]Some trees can drop more than 10 saplings, even limiting to 1 in the file will still drop those amounts (only a few exceptions), not sure why, but if it bothers you, don't take them (☞ﾟヮﾟ)☞

[*]The probability of them dropping saplings sometimes (When the amount is not designated) depends on the size of the tree, trees can be generated from very small to very large (and the sizes in between), if the tree is too small, sometimes it might not drop a sapling, very rare, but it can happen, this is how it works in the game and it is extremely rare to see it, however, it has happened to me testing this mod on 2 or 3 occasions (sh.it, too much luck, I won't get my bets anymore (╯‵□′)╯︵┻━┻).

[*]When I say [b]trees everywhere[/b], this is literal, there is much more variety and quantity in all biomes (and microbiomes) with some exceptions, you will not see the same variety and quantity in the jungle or garden than in the desert or savannah, evidently.

I put special emphasis on [b]more amount of trees[/b], the fact of [b]adding[/b] more variety and [b]restore more trees[/b] has the side effect of populating the maps more, it is an unavoidable effect, if you don't like this, don't bother use this mod.

However, I don't want to seem exaggerated, they come out randomly and they don't always come out with the same amount (but with a variety), personally I think it looks good, it's best that you try it, trees never hurt (and honestly, the jungle is still more exaggerated in amount of trees than what I offer with this mod)

[*][b]The parallaxes[/b] (landscape backgrounds?), each foliage has its parallax, but the game cannot load all the parallaxes of all the foliages available in a biome, for example, [b]forest[/b], has 14 different foliages (15 with the cerebral one) , the game doesn't load 14 parallax, it can't.

Therefore, it is normal to see a parallax that will not coincide with all the foliage that can be seen in the biome, that's how the game works.

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
- As far as I understand, this mod should be compatible with everything, the worst thing that can happen is that some mod you use replaces some of my changes (in the area of trees), or vice versa, without errors or crashes, lots of stems and foliages use custom tags, these are 100% compatible with everything unless they use my same tags (unlikely), but in general this should be compatible with everything.

However, 100% compatible with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2010607826] Betabound![/url] (for example).
