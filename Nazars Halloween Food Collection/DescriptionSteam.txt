[h1]Description, or something like this[/h1]
I simply like the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3358066460] Nazar's Halloween[/url] mod and its small variety of food plates based on pumpkin as the main ingredient, so I created this collection to have the option to collect all those cooking dishes that the mod offers.

34 varied food plates with pumpkin as the main ingredient, [b]integrated into the game’s default cooking collection[/b]. Honestly, I like collectibles—the more collections I can have, the better; for me, it’s one more incentive in games =)

NOTE: No. 29 is “Candy Corn” and it’s the only one that doesn’t have pumpkin as an ingredient nor can it be found with the pumpkin tag, keep that in mind if you notice you’re missing that dish in the collection.
