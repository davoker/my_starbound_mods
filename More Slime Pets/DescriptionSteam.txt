[h1]Description, or something like this[/h1]
Not much to describe here, I saw this mod a long time ago -> [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1384094249] Animated Deco Slime Pets![/url], and I realized that they are not pets, but rather I would describe them as "animated furniture", cute and bouncy.

I wanted to have pets like those but as actual pets, so I got down to work. With a few base sprites for each slime and a few hours of editing, this is the result.

[h1]Content[/h1]
[b]8 types of slimes:[/b]
[list]
[*]Slime King
[*]Slime
[*]Cat
[*]Lava
[*]Poisonous
[*]Rainbow (Or something similar)
[*]Blue
[*]Yellow
[/list]
- The slimes will sleep in their pet house as long as the house is nearby or accessible to them.

- You can purchase this pet's pet house at the Infinity Express store in the Outpost.

- To change the type of slime, simply [b]remove the pet house and place it again[/b]; the slimes will change. This works with all default pets.

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
It should be compatible with everything, it is only a pet to be used with Purchasable Pets.

- If it wasn't clear enough, this mod requires having [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=729438381] Purchasabe Pets[/url] installed, or else it will cause an error and your game won't start.
